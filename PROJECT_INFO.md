## 3. Core Modules & Requirements
<!-- Paste or detail required features and functional requirements -->

### 3.1 Consumer & Account Management
- Account registration, account numbering scheme, classifications (Residential, Commercial, Institutional, etc.)
- Meter serial number tracking, meter status (Active, Disconnected, Under Repair)

### 3.2 Meter Reading & Consumption
- Monthly reading cycles, route/zone grouping
- Previous vs. present reading calculation, high/low consumption alerts

### 3.3 Billing & Rates Engine
- Tiered water rate matrices, minimum charges
- Penalties, environmental fees, arrears, adjustments, due date rules

### 3.4 Collections & Cashiering
- Payment processing, official receipt (OR) generation, partial/advance payments
- Daily collection summaries, cash drawer reconciliation

### 3.5 Reports & Analytics
- Monthly billing summaries, aging of accounts / receivables
- Water production vs. billed consumption, non-revenue water (NRW) indicators

## 4. Technical Specifications & Preferences
- **Target Platform:** WordPress (Content Management System) in future development
- **Current Deliverables:**
  - [**Concept 1**](file:///c:/www/tabuelan-water/concept/1/index.html): Modern Civic Enterprise portal with deep navy hero, bento service cards, side-by-side rates calculator, and advisory ticker.
  - [**Concept 2**](file:///c:/www/tabuelan-water/concept/2/index.html): Welcoming Community Landing Page featuring warm human photography, upfront Featured Announcements & Public Advisories, District Infrastructure Projects & Progress showcase (with impact metrics and completion progress bars), and a bottom Technical & Utility Center hosting the Live Barangay Network Pressure monitor, interactive LWUA Rates & Bill Estimator, and 4-stage application stepper.
  - [**Concept 3**](file:///c:/www/tabuelan-water/concept/3/index.html): "Coastal Community & Eco-Tropical Aqua Modern" story-led portal (deep rainforest/lagoon emerald, vibrant aqua gradients, botanical mist surfaces, and Fraunces + Plus Jakarta Sans typography). Features a full-bleed hero with a live Watershed & Network Pulse telemetry card and "Happening now" strip, a 4-stage Ridge-to-Reef Water Journey & Barangay Community Voices showcase, searchable/filterable newsroom announcements with an advisory reader modal, green infrastructure flagship project with milestone timeline and progress rings, and a 4-tab Concessionaire Toolkit (Coastal/Upland barangay network monitor, LWUA bill estimator with Senior/PWD discount & liter gauge, interactive requirement checklist, and household water-saving tips).
- **Hosting / Environment:** Standard PHP/WordPress compatible hosting
- **Scope Clarification:**
  - ❌ **Out of Scope:** Online bill inquiry / account portal, online payment history database, custom backend programming.
  - ✅ **In Scope:** Public information portal, water rates & tariff guides, Citizen's Charter, water interruption advisories, news & public notices, Transparency Seal & FOI compliance, PhilGEPS bids & procurement archives, downloadable service application forms, payment centers listing, report-a-leak / customer feedback channel.

## 5. Reference Design Analysis (From Other Water Districts)
- Analyzed existing water district portals (Metro Dumaguete Water, Metro Iloilo Water District, Zamboanga City Water District, MCWD Cebu, Davao City Water District).
- **Common structural needs extracted:**
  - Government compliance seals (Philippine Transparency Seal, FOI Philippines, PST Clock).
  - Prominent emergency hotlines & water interruption advisory ticker.
  - Quick action buttons (Apply for Connection, Rates Schedule, Citizen's Charter, Payment Centers, Report a Leak).
  - Modernized card layouts replacing outdated legacy tables and cluttered sidebars.
