How to Apply for a Water Service Connection

Guaranteed standard processing timeline under our Citizen's Charter (RA 11032).

Download Connection Checklist (PDF)
01

Submit Application

Fill out the Service Application Form at the TAWD office in Sitio Ibabao Poblacion with 1 valid ID and proof of property/barangay residency clearance.

02

Site Inspection

Our technical field inspector conducts an on-site survey to verify distance to the nearest distribution main and calculate materials needed within 48 hours.

03

Fee Assessment

Review your official quotation, settle connection fees at the Poblacion cashier, and attend the brief 15-minute consumer orientation on meter care.

04

Meter Installation

Our plumbing crew installs your certified brass meter and connects the service line directly to your residence with active flow testing.
