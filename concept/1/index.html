Water Rates Schedule & Bill Estimator

Tabuelan Water District operates strictly under rates evaluated and approved by the Local Water Utilities Administration (LWUA).

Residential & Domestic Rates (Effective 2026)

Rates apply uniformly across all concessionaires based on meter diameter (1/2" standard residential connection) and monthly volume.

Consumption Bracket Rate (PHP) Remarks
Minimum Charge (0 – 10 cu.m) ₱215.00 Standard flat rate for initial 10 cu.m
11 – 20 cu.m ₱24.50 / cu.m Incremental rate per cubic meter
21 – 30 cu.m ₱28.00 / cu.m Incremental rate per cubic meter
31 cu.m and above ₱32.50 / cu.m High consumption tier
Billing Cycle & Due Dates: Meters are read on a scheduled monthly route. Payment is due within 15 calendar days from billing delivery. A 10% surcharge is imposed on delinquent accounts after due date.

New Concessionaire Guide

How to Apply for a Water Service Connection

Simplified 4-stage process designed to bring clean, potable water directly to your residence or establishment in Tabuelan.

1

Submit Application

Visit the TAWD office or download the application form. Submit with 1 valid government ID and Proof of Ownership or Barangay Clearance.

2

Site Inspection

A TAWD technical inspector surveys your premises to evaluate main line proximity, tapping distance, and necessary piping layout within 2 working days.

3

Fee Assessment & Briefing

Receive the official bill of materials, pay the installation fee and meter deposit at the cashier, and attend the brief orientation on water conservation.

4

Meter Installation

Our plumbing and engineering crew installs the standard water meter and connects the service line. Clean water flows into your property!

📝

Checklist of Requirements for New Service Connection

1x Government Issued ID • Barangay Certificate of Residency • Land Title / Deed of Sale / Consent of Owner • 2x2 ID Photo

Safety First

Strict Compliance with Philippine Drinking Water Standards

Water purity is not just a regulatory obligation; it is our primary commitment to the families and children of Tabuelan.

  • ✓
    Monthly Microbiological Testing: Regular testing for Total Coliforms, Fecal Coliforms/E. coli by accredited DOH laboratories.
  • ✓
    Daily Chlorine Residual Monitoring: Measured daily at various dead-end nodes and distribution sampling points to prevent contamination.
  • ✓
    Physical & Chemical Assessment: Turbidity, pH, and mineral balance evaluated to ensure refreshing taste and crystal clarity.
